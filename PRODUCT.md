# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Our primary users are:
1. **Business Administrators** (Business Owners): Managing a small enterprise (e.g. dishwasher soap manufacture/distribution). They work on laptops and desktop screens, often checking employee check-ins, approving new shops, and monitoring live sales pipelines. They need absolute clarity, ease of management, and robust, reliable data visualization.
2. **Field Salesmen**: Workers on the move visiting small retail shops to collect soap orders. They use mid-to-low range mobile devices in bright daylight. They need a highly visible, hyper-reliable, fast mobile interface that loads instantly and works offline.
3. **Retailers**: Store owners who view their orders on mobile browsers through secure SMS/WhatsApp links. They need a simple, self-explanatory confirmation screen.

## Product Purpose
The app streamlines field sales operation, guarantees salesman physically visited the shop via GPS validation, manages customer accounts, and records order placement and invoice billing. Success is defined by making the salesman's visit frictionless, ensuring high-fidelity location tracking for the admin, and offering immediate invoice transparency.

## Brand Personality
- **Utilitarian**: Efficient, hyper-focused on productivity, zero wasted space.
- **Trustworthy**: Professional, predictable, solid.
- **Frictionless**: Extremely fast transitions, offline-first reliability.

## Anti-references
- Over-decorated, flashy SaaS dashboards that hide actual information behind heavy card outlines and neon gradients.
- Generic "AI templates" with dark-blue backdrops and neon borders.
- Floating popups, heavy animations, and display fonts where data tables and form labels are concerned.

## Design Principles
1. **Familiarity as an Affordance**: Align with category-defining tools (Linear, Raycast) that prioritize keyboard navigability, high density, and clean structure.
2. **Daylight Visibility First**: High contrast contrast ratios and robust typography sizing ensuring legibility in bright field environments.
3. **Restrained but Committed Accentuation**: Use active state color cues solely to direct user workflows, not for decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strict WCAG AA contrast compliance for high-glare field environments.
- Optimized for mobile responsiveness (Capacitor wrapper targets).
- Support for low-bandwidth loading states (Skeleton loaders).
